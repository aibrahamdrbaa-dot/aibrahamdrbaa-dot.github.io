
# Curation and Filtering Standard

The goal is not the largest prompt count. The goal is high-signal material with traceable provenance and evidence.

## Source score — 100 points

- 25 — Official alignment
- 20 — Model specificity
- 15 — Recency
- 20 — Demonstrated evidence
- 10 — Workflow quality
- 10 — Provenance/licensing clarity

## Prompt-level filter

Retain a recipe when it has at least four of:
- clear artifact/goal;
- meaningful composition;
- subject-specific detail;
- useful lighting/material decisions;
- model-aware instructions;
- reference/edit invariants;
- explicit text requirements where needed;
- evidence that the prompt was used;
- reusable structure.

## Reject or quarantine

- outdated model IDs;
- generic master-prompt claims without evidence;
- unclear-provenance prompt dumps;
- adjective density without control value;
- instructions contradicting the current API;
- API-specific limitations presented as universal model limits;
- hidden unavailable assets;
- unclear licensing for redistribution.

## Evidence labels

- OFFICIAL — provider documentation/model guidance.
- CURATED — maintained third-party library with provenance/output evidence.
- COMMUNITY — practitioner material, not authoritative.
- INFERRED — synthesis from multiple sources; never present as provider policy.

## Package policy

Official guidance is the authority. Maintained model-specific skills supply workflow structure. Community galleries are used to discover patterns, not to repackage third-party prompt bodies as owned content.
